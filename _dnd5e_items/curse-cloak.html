Curse Cloak
Wondrous Item
Weight
4
Proficiency
—
A

While attuned to and wearing this cloak, you regain hit points from necrotic damage instead of taking damage, regaining a number of hit points equal to the necrotic damage dealt.

Any spell that would restore hit points to you instead deals damage to you equal to the amount of hit points the spell would have restored. This damage is of the same type as the spell.

In addition, you have vulnerability to radiant damage.
