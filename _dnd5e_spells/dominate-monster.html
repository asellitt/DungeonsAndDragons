Dominate Monster
8th-level Enchantment
Casting Time
1 action
Range
60 feet
Components
V, S
Duration
1 minute
C

Target must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw or be charmed. If you or targets that are friendly to you are fighting it, it has advantage on the saving throw.

While charmed, you have a telepathic link while on the same plane of existence. You can use this to issue commands (no action required), which it does its best to obey. If the target completes the order, it defends and preserves itself to the best of its ability.

Spend an action to take complete control of the target. Until the end of your next turn, the target takes only the actions you choose. During this time, you can cause the target to use reactions, but this uses your own reaction.

Each time the target takes damage, it makes a Wisdom saving throw. If successful, the spell ends.

At Higher Levels
When you cast this spell with a 9th-level spell slot, the duration is concentration, up to 8 hours.
