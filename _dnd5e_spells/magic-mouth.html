Magic Mouth
2nd-level Illusion (ritual)
Casting Time
1 minute
Range
30 feet
Components
V, S, M
Duration
Instantaneous

Material: jade dust worth 10GP, which the spell consumes.

Choose an object that you can see and that isn't being worn or carried by another creature. Then speak the message (25 words or fewer), though it can be delivered over as long as 10 minutes. Finally, determine the trigger for the spell to deliver your message.

On trigger, a magical mouth appears on the object and recites the message in your voice and at the same volume you spoke. When you cast this spell, you can have the spell end after it delivers its message, or it can repeat its message whenever the trigger occurs.

The trigger must be based on visual or audible conditions that occur within 30 feet of the object.

At Higher Levels
When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 2nd level or higher, the spell creates one more dart for each slot level above 1st.
