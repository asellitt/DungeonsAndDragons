Glyph Of Warding
3rd-level Abjuration
Casting Time
1 hour
Range
Touch
Components
V, S, M
Duration
Until triggered

Material: powdered diamond worth 200 GP, which the spell consumes.

Trigger. You decide what triggers the glyph when you cast the spell. Once triggered, this spell ends. You can refine the trigger so that only specific creatures activate it, or specify conditions that must be met for the glyph to be triggered.

Explosive Rune. When triggered, each creature in a 20 ft. radius Sphere makes a Dexterity saving throw. Creatures take 5d8 Acid, Cold, Fire, Lightning, or Thunder damage on a failed save or half as much damage on a successful one.

Spell Glyph. Store a prepared spell of level 3 or lower in the glyph by casting it as part of creating the glyph. The spell must target a single creature or an area. The spell being stored has no immediate effect when cast in this way.

At Higher Levels
The damage increases by 1d8 for each spell slot level above 3. Or, you can store any spell of up to the same level as the spell slot you use for the Glyph of Warding.
