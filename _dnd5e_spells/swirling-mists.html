Swirling Mists
1st-level Conjuration
Casting Time
1 action
Range
60 feet
Components
V, S
Duration
Instantaneous

Swirling mist forms around a creature of your choice within range, and then forces itself into the creatures lungs. The target must make a Constitution saving throw. The target takes 4d6 radiant damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one.

At Higher Levels
When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 2nd level or higher, the damage increases by 1d6 for each slot level above 1st.
