Modify Memory
5th-level Enchantment
Casting Time
1 action
Range
30 feet
Components
V, S
Duration
1 minute
C

The target makes a Wisdom saving throw. If you are fighting the target, it has advantage. On a failed save, the target becomes charmed and incapacitated. If it takes any damage or is targeted by another spell, this spell ends, and none of the target's memories are modified.

While charmed, you can affect 10 minutes of the target's memory of an event within the last 24 hours.

The target must be able to understand you for the memories to take root. Its mind fills in any gaps in the details of your description. If the spell ends before you finish the description, the targets's memory isn't altered.

Remove curse or greater restoration restores the target's memories.

At Higher Levels
You can alter the target's memories up to 7 days ago (6th level), 30 days ago (7th level), 1 year ago (8th level), or any time (9th level).
