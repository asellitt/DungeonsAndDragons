Intellect Fortress
3rd-level Abjuration
Casting Time
1 action
Range
30 feet
Components
V
Duration
1 hour
C

For the duration, you or one willing creature you can see within range has resistance to psychic damage, as well as advantage on Intelligence, Wisdom, and Charisma saving throws.

At Higher Levels
You can target one additional creature for each slot level above 3rd. The creatures must be within 30 feet of each other when you target them.
