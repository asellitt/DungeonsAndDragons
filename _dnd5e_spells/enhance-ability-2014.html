Enhance Ability
2nd-level Transmutation
Casting Time
1 action
Range
Touch
Components
V, S, M
Duration
1 hour
C

Material: fur or a feather.

Bear's Endurance. Target has advantage on Constitution checks. It also gains 2d6 temporary hit points, which are lost when the spell ends.

Bull's Strength. Target has advantage on Strength checks, and his or her carrying capacity doubles.

Cat's Grace. Target has advantage on Dexterity checks. No fall damage from 20 ft or less, if not incapacitated.

Eagle's Splendor. Target has advantage on Charisma checks.

Fox's Cunning. Target has advantage on Intelligence checks.

Owl's Wisdom. Target has advantage on Wisdom checks.

At Higher Levels
When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 3rd level or higher, you can target one additional creature for each slot level above 2nd.
