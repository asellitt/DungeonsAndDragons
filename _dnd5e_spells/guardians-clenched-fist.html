Guardian's Clenched Fist
5th-level Evocation
Casting Time
1 action
Range
60 feet
Components
V, S
Duration
1 minute
C

You create a Large hand of translucent mist. It has AC 20 and HP equal to your maximum HP. If it drops to 0 HP, the spell ends.

When you cast the spell and as a bonus action on your turns, you can move the hand up to 60 feet and then cause one effect with it.

Clenched Fist. The hand strikes one creature or object within 5 feet of it. Make a melee spell attack for the hand using your game statistics. On a hit, the target takes 4d8 force damage.

At Higher Levels
When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 6th level or higher, the damage from the clenched fist option increases by 2d8 for each slot level above 5th.
