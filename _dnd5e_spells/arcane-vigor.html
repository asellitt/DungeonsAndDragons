Arcane Vigor
2nd-level Abjuration
Casting Time
1 bonus action
Range
Touch
Components
V,S
Duration
Instantaneous

You tap into your life force to heal yourself. Roll one or two of your unexpended Hit Point Dice, and re- gain a number of Hit Points equal to the rail's total plus your spellcasting ability modifier. Those dice are then expended.

At Higher Levels
The number of unexpended Hit Dice you can reroll increases by one for each spell slot level above 2.
