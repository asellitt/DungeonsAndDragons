Alter Self
2nd-level Transmutation
Casting Time
1 action
Range
Self
Components
V,S
Duration
1 hour
C

You can take a Magic action to replace the option you chose with a different one.

Aquatic Adaptation. You can breathe underwater and gain a Swim Speed equal to your Speed.

Change Appearance. You alter your appearance, including your species, height, weight, facial features, sound of your voice, hair length, coloration, and other distinguishing characteristics. None of your statistics change. You can't appear as a creature of a different size, and your basic shape stays the same; if you're bipedal, you can't use this spell to become quadrupedal, for instance. For the duration, you can take a Magic action to change your appearance in this way again.

Natural Weapons. You grow claws (Slashing), fangs (Piercing), horns (Piercing), or hooves (Bludgeoning). Your Unarmed Strike deals 1d6 damage with that new growth, and you use your spellcasting ability modifier for the attack and damage rolls rather than using Strength.
