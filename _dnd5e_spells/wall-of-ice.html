Wall of Ice
6th-level Evocation
Casting Time
1 action
Range
120 feet
Components
V, S, M
Duration
10 minutes
C

Material: a small piece of quartz.

You create a wall of ice and can form it into a hemispherical dome or a sphere with a radius of up to 10 feet, or a surface of ten 10-foot-square contiguous panels.

Creatures within its area are pushed to one side of the wall and must make a Dexterity saving throw, taking 10d6 cold damage, or half on success.

Each 10-foot section has AC 12 and 30 hit points, and is vulnerable to fire damage. Destroying a section of wall leaves behind a sheet of frigid air. A creature moving through the sheet of frigid air for the first time on a turn must make a Constitution saving throw, taking 5d6 cold damage on a failed save, or half on success.

At Higher Levels
When using a slot of 7th level or higher, the damage when it appears increases by 2d6, and the damage from the frigid air increases by 1d6, for each slot level above 6th.
