Elementalism
Transmutation cantrip
Casting Time
1 action
Range
30 feet (5-ft cube)
Components
V, S
Duration
Instantaneous
  • Beckon Air. Create a strong breeze to ripple cloth, stir dust, rustle leaves, and close open doors and shutters, in a 5ft cube. Doors and shutters being held open by someone or something aren't affected.
  • Beckon Earth. Create a thin shroud of dust or sand that covers surfaces in a 5ft-square area, or cause a single word to appear in your handwriting in a patch of dirt or sand.
  • Beckon Fire. Create a thin cloud of harmless embers and colored, scented smoke in a 5ft cube. Choose the color and scent. The embers can light candles, torches, or lamps in that area. The scent lingers for 1 min.
  • Beckon Water. Create a spray of cool mist that lightly dampens creatures and objects in a 5ft cube. Or, create 1 cup of clean water either in an open container or on a surface, and the water evaporates in 1 min.
  • Sculpt Element. Cause dirt, sand, fire, smoke, mist, or water that can fit in a 1ft cube to assume a crude shape for 1 hr.
