Dominate Person
5th-level Enchantment
Casting Time
1 action
Range
60 feet
Components
V, S
Duration
1 minute
C

The humanoid target must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw or be charmed by. If you or your allies fighting it, it has advantage on the saving throw.

While charmed, you have a telepathic link with it as long as you are on the same plane. You can use this telepathic link to issue commands (no action required). After finishing an order, the target defends itself.

You can use your action to take total control of the target. Until the end of your next turn, the creature takes only the actions you choose. You can use your reaction to cause the creature to use a reaction.

Any time the target takes damage, it makes a new Wisdom saving hrow. On success, the spell ends.

At Higher Levels
For a 6th level slot, the duration increases to 10 minutes. For a 7th level slot, the duration increases to 1 hour. For an 8th or higher slot, the duration increases to 8 hours.
