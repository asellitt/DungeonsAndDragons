Enlarge/Reduce
2nd-level Transmutation
Casting Time
1 action
Range
30 feet
Components
V, S, M
Duration
1 minute
C

Material: a pinch of powdered iron.

A creature or an object you can see within range to grow or shrink for the duration. Unwilling targets can make a Constitution saving throw. On a success, the spell has no effect.

Enlarge. The target's size increases by one category. The target has Advantage on Strength checks saving throws. The target's attacks with its enlarged weapons or Unarmed Strikes deal an extra 1d4 damage on a hit.

Reduce. The target's size decreases by one category. The target has Disadvantage on Strength checks saving throws. The target's attacks with its reduced weapons or Unarmed Strikes deal 1d4 less damage on a hit (this can't reduce the damage below 1).
