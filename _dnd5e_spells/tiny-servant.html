Tiny Servant
3rd-level Transmutation
Casting Time
1 minute
Range
Touch
Components
V, S
Duration
8 hours

You touch one Tiny, nonmagical object that isn't attached to another object or a surface and isn't being carried by another creature. The target animates and sprouts little arms and legs, becoming a creature under your control until the spell ends or the creature drops to 0 hit points.

As a bonus action, you can mentally command the creature within 120 ft. of you. You decide what action the creature will take and where it will move, or issue a simple, general command. If you issue no commands, the servant does nothing other than defend itself. The servant continues to follow that order until its task is complete.

When the creature drops to 0 hit points, it reverts to its original form, and any remaining damage carries over to that form.

At Higher Levels
You can animate two additional objects for each slot level above 3rd.
