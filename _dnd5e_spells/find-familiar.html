Find Familiar
1st-level Conjuration (ritual)
Casting Time
1 hour
Range
90 feet
Components
V, S, M
Duration
Instantaneous

Material: 10 gp worth of charcoal, incense, and herbs that must be consumed by fire in a brass brazier.

Telepathic Connection. While within 100 feet, you can communicate with the familiar telepathically.
Bonus Action: see/hear through the familiar until the start of your next turn, gain the benefits of any special senses.
Reaction: deliver touch spells through the familiar.

Combat. It rolls its own Initiative and acts on its own turn. It can't attack, but it can take other actions as normal.

Disappearance. When it drops to 0 HP, it disappears. It reappears after you cast this spell again. As a Magic action, you can temporarily dismiss the familiar to a pocket dimension. Alternatively, you can dismiss it forever. As a Magic action while it is temporarily dismissed, you can cause it to reappear in an unoccupied space within 30 feet of you. Whenever the familiar drops to 0 Hit Points or disappears into the pocket dimension, it leaves behind in its space anything it was wearing or carrying.
