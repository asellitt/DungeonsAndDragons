Spare the Dying
Necromancy cantrip
Casting Time
1 action
Range
15 feet
Components
V, S
Duration
Instantaneous
Choose a creature within range that has 0 Hit Points and isn't dead. The creature becomes Stable.
At Higher Levels
The range doubles when you reach levels 5 (30 feet), 11 (60 feet), and 17 (120 feet).
