Command
1st-level Enchantment
Casting Time
1 action
Range
60 feet
Components
V
Duration
1 round

You speak a one-word command to a creature you can see within range. The target must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw or follow the command on its next turn. The spell has no effect if the target is undead, doesn't understand the language, or the command is directly harmful to it.

  • Approach: The target moves direclty toward you, ending its turn if it moves within 5 feet of you.
  • Drop: The target drops whatever it is holding and ends its turn.
  • Flee: The target spends its turn moving away from you by the fastest available means.
  • Grovel: The target falls prone and ends its turn.
  • Halt: The target doesn't move and takes no actions. Flying creatures stays aloft, provided it's able to do so.
At Higher Levels
When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 2nd level or higher, you can affect one additional creature within 30 feet for each slot level above 1st.
