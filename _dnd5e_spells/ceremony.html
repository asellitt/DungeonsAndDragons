Ceremony
1st-level Abjuration (ritual)
Casting Time
1 hour
Range
Touch
Components
V, S, M
Duration
Instantaneous

Material: 25 gp worth of powdered silver, which the spell consumes.

You perform a special religious ceremony, the target must be within 10 feet of you.

  • Atonement: Make a DC 20 Wisdom (Insight) check. On success, you restore the target to its original alignment.
  • Bless Water: Convert one vial of water in to holy water.
  • Coming of Age: Once, for the next 24 hours, whenever the target makes an ability check, add a d4 to the total.
  • Dedication: Once, for the next 24 hours, whenever the target makes a saving throw, add a d4 to the total.
  • Funeral Rite: For the next 7 days, the corpse can't become undead by any means short of a wish spell.
  • Wedding: For the next 7 days, each target gains a +2 bonus to AC while they are within 30 feet of each other. A creature can benefit from this rite again only if widowed.
